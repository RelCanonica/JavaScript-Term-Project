------------------------------
    GAME DESCRIPTIONS
------------------------------
1)  Cat Explore: Use the Up, Down, Left, and Right buttons to guide kitty through the living room on her way to knock a knicknack off the shelf. Avoid the empty shelves by fitting through the gaps.

2)  Purrzzles is a puzzle game. You click and drag puzzle pieces to fit the shape of the outline provided to form a picture of a cat. 

3)  Feline Facts is a quiz game. For each question, you select an answer. If you answer correctly, you earn 1 point. The goal is to earn as many points as possible, with the highest score being 7 points.
